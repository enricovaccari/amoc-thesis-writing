\chapter*{Declaration of Authorship}
\addcontentsline{toc}{chapter}{Declaration of Authorship}

I declare that this thesis is my own work and that all sources of information and ideas have
been fully acknowledged. Where the work of others has been drawn on, it is cited in the text
and listed in the references. This thesis has not been submitted, in whole or in part, for
any other degree or qualification.

Artificial-intelligence assistants were used in the preparation of this thesis: to support
drafting and refining text, to structure arguments, to write and debug analytical code, to
locate candidate literature that I then verified and read at first hand, and to produce a
first pass of the coding of participant responses, which I reviewed and adjudicated item by
item. The research design, the interpretation of the results, the methodological judgements
and the final wording are my own. The role of these tools, and the boundaries I kept around
it, is documented in full in Appendix~\ref{app:ai-use}.
