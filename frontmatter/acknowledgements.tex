\chapter*{Acknowledgements}
\addcontentsline{toc}{chapter}{Acknowledgements}

I should say, before anything else, that I have always been better at expressing gratitude through actions and presence than through words. So let these few lines stand for something I hope I show more fully in how I am with the people in them.

My first thanks go to my supervisor, Jonathan, who welcomed an eclectic, unconventional idea and let it grow. It means something to me that he is an astrophysicist teaching machine learning: living proof that one can be deeply competent without being narrow, and that curiosity is allowed to move sideways as well as down.

To my parents, whose support has been infinite for as long as I can remember: you gave me curiosity, method, and the freedom to become who I am. Almost everything I value, I first saw in you.

To my brother, who does not live with me anymore but whom I feel close all the same. You have always been a source of inspiration to me — for your empathy, your uncommon goodness, and above all your discipline. I cannot imagine my life without your presence in it.

To my grandparents, who helped raise me and passed on love and compassion as if they were the most natural things to teach. I am beyond grateful to have had you in my life, and I hope to carry your example forward.

To my friends, who stood by me through these years and embraced a path and a vision that run against the current. Walking an unconventional road is far lighter with people who choose to walk part of it beside you.

To the people who inspire me every day without knowing it, and whom I have never met: you remind me what it looks like to think broadly, to keep learning, and to try to matter.

To my coursemates, to everyone who took part in the study, and more generally to the people who have enriched my life over these last years, this work carries something of all of you in it.

Last, and not least, I want to thank myself: for the tenacity to hold on through the difficult moments and the self-doubt, for keeping faith with my values, for the times I managed to inspire others, and for continuing to drive myself toward the future I want to build, one where I can become fully myself and help make the world a better place, for humans and non-humans alike.
