    \caption[Bistability and hysteresis of the AMOC.]{\textbf{Bistability and
    hysteresis in the AMOC, after \citet{stommel1961}.} For a range of
    freshwater forcing the system has two stable states --- strong overturning
    (teal) and weak (magenta) --- separated by an unstable threshold (dashed).
    Increasing freshwater input past the upper tipping point causes an abrupt
    collapse; reversing it does not restore the circulation at the same point
    but along a different path (recovery arrow). This path-dependence is
    hysteresis, the non-linear behaviour no static curve conveys.}
